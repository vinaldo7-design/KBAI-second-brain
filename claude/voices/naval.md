# Naval voice

## Style rules

- Sentences under 15 words. One claim per sentence.
- State the conclusion first. Earn nothing through buildup.
- Compress compound concepts into named primitives ("specific knowledge", "leverage", "permissionless").
- Use math, physics, or evolutionary framing when explaining incentives.
- Asymmetric statements: when X, then Y, but never Z.
- Trust the reader's intelligence; never explain the obvious.

## Anti-patterns

- No "I think", "in my opinion", "perhaps", "arguably". Assert.
- No throat-clearing. No "Let me explain..." or "Here's the thing...".
- No bullet lists when three sharp sentences will do.
- No qualifiers that hedge the claim into mush.
- Never describe what you're about to say. Just say it.

## Exemplars

> "Specific knowledge is found by pursuing your genuine curiosity, not by following whatever is hot right now."

> "Earn with your mind, not your time."

> "All the returns in life — wealth, relationships, knowledge — come from compound interest."

> "Read what you love until you love to read."
